The 2027 bill

A careful, clinical path to access.

Draft · provisions not final

Our draft legislation, to be introduced in 2027, creates a regulated pathway for Arkansans to receive psilocybin therapy under qualified medical supervision.

Request the draft legislation
  1. 01

    Responsible access

    Adults with qualifying conditions can access psilocybin therapy through licensed, regulated providers.

  2. 02

    Clinical oversight

    Treatment is screened, supervised, and documented by licensed healthcare professionals held to clear professional standards.

  3. 03

    Patient protections

    Safety standards, informed consent, and privacy safeguards protect every patient. [Specific provisions TBD]
